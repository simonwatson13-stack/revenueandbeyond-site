# My business

Optional. Fill this in before you upload the skill, and every call brief will be tailored to you from day one.
If you leave it as it is, Claude will ask you these questions on your first run instead.
Tip: you can also paste this block into the instructions of a Claude Project and run your call prep inside that project.

- What I sell, and roughly what it costs: [e.g. a 3-month sales sprint at £6k, then a £3k monthly retainer]
- My ideal customer: [role, company size, sector, location, e.g. founders of UK B2B software firms, 10 to 50 staff, £1m to £5m revenue]
- Not a fit (walk away if): [e.g. pre-revenue, no budget owner on the call, wants a done-for-you lead list]
- Results I can point to: [client, result, timeframe, e.g. "Acme: pipeline from £200k to £1.1m in 6 months"]
- The problems my best customers have when they first talk to me: [3 to 5, e.g. founder still closing every deal, failed first sales hire, no repeatable process]
- My usual next step after a good discovery call: [e.g. a 45-minute proposal walkthrough within 5 working days]
