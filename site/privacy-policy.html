Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 September 2026

This page is a plain-English description of what actually happens on revenueandbeyond.com today. It isn't legal advice, and it's worth a solicitor's check before you rely on it for compliance purposes.

Who this covers

This policy covers revenueandbeyond.com, run by Simon Watson, trading as Revenue & Beyond, based in London, UK. If you have a question about it, email simon@revenueandbeyond.com.

What information is collected

revenueandbeyond.com itself has no sign-up form, account system, or checkout, so it doesn't collect or store any personal data directly. Two things on the site hand you off to a third party, and it's their form that collects your details:

  • Newsletter sign-up. The subscribe box on this site is a Beehiiv form. If you sign up, your email address is collected and stored by Beehiiv, not by this site, and it's used to send you the newsletter.
  • Booking a call. Every "book a call" or "talk strategy" link on this site takes you to Calendly, in a new tab, to pick a time. Calendly collects whatever you enter there (name, email, and any notes) directly, not through this site.

Simon reads and replies to his own inbox and calendar personally, so any details you share when you book a call or email him directly are used only to prepare for and hold that conversation.

Cookies and tracking

This site doesn't run analytics or advertising trackers of its own. The one script it loads is Beehiiv's attribution tag, which tags a newsletter sign-up with where you came from if you go on to subscribe. Beehiiv and Calendly may set their own cookies once you're on their pages, governed by their own policies (linked below). The site is hosted on Netlify, which may log standard technical data (like IP address) for hosting and security purposes, as most web hosts do.

Third-party services used on this site

  • Beehiiv (newsletter) — beehiiv.com/privacy
  • Calendly (booking) — calendly.com/privacy
  • Netlify (hosting) — netlify.com/privacy

How long information is kept

Newsletter and booking data is held according to Beehiiv's and Calendly's own retention rules. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email, and you can cancel or reschedule a booking through Calendly directly. Emails sent to Simon are kept as long as needed to answer them and keep a record of the conversation.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you can ask to see, correct, or delete the personal data held about you, or object to how it's used. For your newsletter or booking data, the quickest route is directly through Beehiiv or Calendly's own tools, or by emailing Simon and asking him to pass on a request. You can also complain to the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at any time.

Changes to this policy

If what the site collects or how it's used changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
