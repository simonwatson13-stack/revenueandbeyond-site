Questions

What does the fee cover?

The retainer is flat because the scope isn't. It flexes around whatever the business needs next, whether that's pipeline, pricing, how the business runs day to day, or where a hire is overdue. There is no performance component in this tier.

How is this different from Founder Line?

Founder Line is a monthly call with message access in between. Retained Partner is ongoing involvement across the business, with direct access between sessions.

When would a performance component make sense?

When there's a specific trigger, such as a funded growth push or a pricing change already in motion. That's the Retainer + Performance tier.
