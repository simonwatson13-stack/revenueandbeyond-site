Founder Line is delivered jointly with Jordan Adams. Each month's call is with me or Jordan. His background and the full detail are at thefounderline.com.

What's the difference between Founder Line and Retained Partner?

Founder Line is a monthly call with message access in between, at £600 + VAT a month. Retained Partner is ongoing strategic involvement across commercial, operational and financial priorities, with direct access between sessions, at £3,500 + VAT a month.
