69% of buyers still want a human

They're not calling to find out who you are. They're calling to check they were right about you.

By Simon Watson · 22 Nov 2026

Most founders I speak to think the sale starts when the meeting gets booked or the phone rings asking for more info…

It doesn't!

By the time a buyer gets in touch, more than half of the buying decision is done.

6sense asked nearly 4,000 B2B buyers how they bought last year. On average, they made first contact 61% of the way through their journey. And four times out of five, the deal went to the supplier who was already their favourite before that first conversation.

So when a buyer books a call with you, they're not calling to find out who you are. They're calling to check they were right about you.

That's worth thinking about as you plan 2027. Because the work that wins the deal now happens in rooms you're not in.

They ask AI. 6sense found 94% of buyers use tools like ChatGPT somewhere along the way.

They ask each other. In a study of 1,202 US decision-makers, 73% trusted recommendations from peers, ahead of vendor websites at 55%.

And they check whether your website and your pitch tell the same story.

Here's the bit I love. Gartner found two thirds of buyers say they'd rather buy without a salesperson. But 69% still go back to a human to check what AI told them. They don't want a "rep". They want someone who actually knows the work.

For a B2B founder, that's you. You're still the best salesperson in your business... you just need buyers to find you before they pick up the phone.

So try this before Friday. Open ChatGPT and ask it two questions.

1) "Who are the best [at what you do] for [who you serve] in the UK/US/UAE etc?"

2) "What is [your company] known for?"

After that, read your own homepage as if you'd never met yourself.

Twenty minutes, and you'll know exactly what a buyer sees.
