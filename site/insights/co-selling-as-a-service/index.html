Co-Selling as a Service

Technical founders make brilliant salespeople, once they've been shown how to do it.

By Simon Watson · 15 Nov 2026

Closing the next £100k.

Incredible product, paying customers, but she’d never done sales before becoming a founder, and she was ready for someone to show her how to sell it with confidence…

This is exactly what a founder said to me last week.

She went on to say… “I don’t need more tutorials or playbooks. What I need is someone in the deals with me, side by side, showing me what to do, covering my blind spots, helping me see where I’m dropping the ball, and helping me close more contracts and bring in new customers. Another £100k this quarter would put me back on track.”

It stopped me dead in my tracks.

We unpicked it a bit more, to fully understand what she meant, and it resonated with other conversations I’ve been part of. She is technically brilliant (a PhD, no less) and knows the product better than most, but really struggles when it comes to the sales side of things. Often she didn’t back herself, which meant a drop in confidence, and because sales was all new to her, she always found the sales process a little “icky”.

I know first-hand that technical founders make brilliant salespeople, once they’ve been shown how to do it and have a process wrapped around it.

And this got me thinking...

Hundreds of sales coaches and consultants on LinkedIn all claim to add hundreds of thousands in top-line revenue, but very few have “skin in the game”, and even fewer are offering to roll up their sleeves, get their hands dirty, close deals and share in the rewards...

So I am running a test...

I want to speak to founders who DON’T need another playbook or another script, but who DO need someone in their corner to take the load, get hands-on, work deals directly across each stage, close the next £100k in revenue with them and share in the profits.

I'm calling it "Co-Selling as a Service".

So I'm opening up a few slots. It's a test, and it's first come, first served. Hit reply, or DM me, and we'll have a chat about what this actually looks like.
