Why Your Startup's Sales Aren't Scaling (and How to Fix It)

A SeedLegals session with Anthony Rose on why founder-led sales stall, and how to build a sales engine that does not depend on you.

By Simon Watson · 27 Jul 2026

A 53-minute SeedLegals session, hosted by Anthony Rose, co-founder and CEO of SeedLegals, with me as the guest.

Founders usually drive the early sales. Growth then stalls once the founder becomes the bottleneck. We cover why that happens, how to build a repeatable lead generation and sales engine, when to hire your first salesperson, and the systems that let a company scale beyond founder-led selling.
