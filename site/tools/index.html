Questions

What is a Claude skill?

A folder of instructions and files that teaches Claude how to do one job your way, every time. You add it to Claude once and it is there whenever you ask for that job.

What do I need to use a kit?

A paid Claude plan (Pro or above) and ten minutes. Each kit comes with a one-page set-up guide. Some kits work better with your call recorder or email connected to Claude, and the guide says which.

Can I share a kit with my team?

Use it across your own business as much as you like. Please don't resell it or share it outside your company.

What if it doesn't work for me?

Email me at simon@revenueandbeyond.com. I'll help you get it running, and if I can't, I'll refund you.

What does done with you mean?

I build the system into your business on your own tools, test it with you and hand it over working. It starts with a free scoping call so we both know the fixed fee before anything starts.
