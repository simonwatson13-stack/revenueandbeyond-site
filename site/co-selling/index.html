Questions

Who is it for?

B2B founders who have proved their product sells, with at least £100k in revenue, and who need help finding and closing more deals. Services and tech, UK and UAE. It isn't for pre-revenue or idea-stage businesses.

Do you bring your own leads?

No. I work your pipeline with you, and we build more of it together: your network and old pipeline first, then inbound and outbound. If you want meetings booked for you, that's the cold calling add-on.

How does the 15% work?

It applies to new revenue from deals we work on together, for the first 12 months of each contract. Deals already at proposal or contract stage when we start are excluded. You pay it when your client pays you. Depending on your business, we may base it on gross profit instead, and we agree that up front.

How much of your time do I get?

A weekly pipeline review, up to two prospect or client calls a week together, and unlimited access in between. Decks and proposals run alongside, AI-assisted, in your own Claude.

Do you promise a number?

No. We agree the target together once I've seen your pipeline, and the 90 days are built around hitting it. The shared upside option is how I put my money where my mouth is.
