The call

What it is, and what it is not.

What it is

  • A proper conversation about where the business is and what is holding revenue back
  • My read on where the real leverage is in your commercial engine
  • What I learned building and exiting three businesses (IPO, trade sale, VC-backed sale)
  • Time for whatever questions you have
  • A write-up of the call, plus a free commercial report on the first steps to make your revenue predictable and your business exit-ready

What it is not

  • A sales pitch dressed up as advice
  • A qualification form or a slide deck
  • A template. The report is built for your business, not lifted from someone else's
  • An obligation. If there is a fit to work together, you'll feel it

Before we speak

Three things that make the call worth it.

  1. Bring rough numbers

    Revenue, how many deals you still close yourself, and where the pipeline comes from. Rough is fine.

  2. Bring the real worry

    Not the polished version. The thing that comes back every time you try to step away from the business.

  3. Bring your questions

    About pricing, hiring, process, or what a buyer would see. Ask me anything and expect a straight answer.
